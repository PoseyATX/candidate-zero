# Decision: Candidate Zero means start at Zero

## Context
Fat kit, camp mall, free weekly cards, 24 well-seated personas, and a 3-step form presented as a "hook" violated the product name.

## Decision
1. **Day-one personas: 4 only** — Teacher, Veteran, Preacher, Feed-Store. Not well-seated. PA_* unlock later.
2. **First-run deck: legs + voice** — PL01 + persona signature. Ballot doors camp-only.
3. **Career deck** is the legacy. Scars on loss.
4. **No free weekly card** in Zero. Opportunities optional (walk past).
5. **Zero camp: ballot doors only** pre-ballot. No Camp actions mall (no BUY/MV/CH/AL dump). Empty camp is correct. Earned HK threads only when live.
6. **Opening is one immersive beat** — four people, clerk's window, walk in and file. Defaults for place (open/east/taxes). Not a 3-step wizard.
7. **Hand tight (5–7).** Splash/act ceremony untouched.
8. **Harness** uses `starterKit: 'harness'`.

## Consequences
- Week one is doors + legs + voice.
- Filing does not unlock free verbs.
- Pages is the product authority.
